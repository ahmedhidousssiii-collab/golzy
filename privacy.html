Golzy: Live Soccer Scores

Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Applies to Golzy for iPhone

The short version

  • Golzy does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • The teams and competitions you follow, your match predictions and your settings are saved only on your iPhone.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Scores and stats are downloaded from a public sports data feed; we don't send it anything about you.

Who we are

Golzy is a football (soccer) live-score app developed by Mohamed Idris Merbai ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Golzy has no sign-up or login and asks for no personal details. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other personal data on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your preferences, the app saves the following in its own storage on your iPhone: the teams, competitions and matches you follow, your "Who will win?" picks, and your settings (theme, clock format, alert types). A copy of the teams you follow is shared with Golzy's own widgets on the same device so they can show your teams' matches. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Scores and sports data

To show live scores, fixtures, tables, lineups, player pages and news, the app downloads publicly available sports data and team crests directly from ESPN's public web services. These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the data (for example a date, a league or a match ID). Like any website you visit, those servers can see your device's IP address. We don't receive or store this information. News articles open in an in-app browser at the publisher's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, kick-off reminders and goal and full-time alerts are created on your iPhone by the app itself. Live Activities (the live score on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island) are also updated on the device. You can turn notifications off at any time in Settings.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

This version of Golzy shows no ads and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software from third parties. It does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Golzy changes how information is handled (for example by adding ads, optional purchases or server-sent alerts), we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: see the support page.
