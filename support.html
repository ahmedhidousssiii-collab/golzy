Frequently asked questions

How do I get goal alerts?

Follow a team (tap ☆ on its page or in the Following tab), or tap the bell on any match. Then make sure notifications are allowed for Golzy in iOS Settings → Notifications.

How do I put a match on my Lock Screen?

Open a match that hasn't finished and tap the pin icon at the top. The live score appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Golzy and choose "My Teams". It shows live scores and next matches for the teams you follow.

Can I watch matches in the app?

No. Golzy shows scores, stats and alerts. It doesn't stream video.

Which languages are supported?

English, French and Arabic. The app follows your iPhone's language.
